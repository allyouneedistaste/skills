# Notices

`skills/runway-site/references/craft.md` takes ideas from the craft rules of
[Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) by Paul Bakaus (Apache 2.0).
The wording is ours and no Impeccable file is included. `craft.md` suggests running
`npx impeccable`; if you do, it is downloaded then, under its own licence.

Fonts are fetched from Google Fonts when a site is built and are not included: check
each family's licence (almost all are OFL). Pictures come from whichever image tool
you use, under its terms; `assets/example-tipped.jpg` is our own example, with AI-generated pictures.
